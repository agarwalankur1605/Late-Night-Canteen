RU Night Bites — Late Night Food Delivery, Rishihood University

The Problem - 
Students at Rishihood University are required to be in their hostels by 11 PM. But staying up late is common — and hunger hits hard after midnight. The only options available inside the hostels are overpriced chips and instant noodles from a tuck shop.
Chai Adda, the campus food store, has a full menu — Maggi, sandwiches, cold coffee, snacks, drinks — but it is completely inaccessible to students after curfew. Chai Adda also earns zero revenue during these late night hours despite having the kitchen, staff, and ingredients available.


The Solution - 
RU Night Bites is a two-sided web platform that connects students to Chai Adda during night hours (11 PM – 2 AM).

Students browse the Chai Adda menu, add items to cart, pay via UPI or cash, and track their order live
Chai Adda staff receive orders on a dedicated admin dashboard, confirm payment, prepare food, and dispatch it
A delivery person does fixed rounds to R1, R2, and R3 hostel receptions at 11:45 PM, 12:30 AM, 1:15 AM, and 2:00 AM
Students collect their food from the reception desk (manned by guards all night) and pay cash there if they chose cash on delivery


Login with university email and password
Pay via UPI or select cash on delivery
Receive a digital bill with unique order ID after payment confirmation


Admin Side - 
Dedicated password-protected page 
Confirmation panel — see incoming orders, verify UPI payment, accept or reject
Full order history and revenue tracking

Tech Stack - HTML , CSS , JAVASCRIPT , REACT

How to Run - 
Clone this repository
Open the folder in VS Code
Right click on index.html → Open with Live Server
